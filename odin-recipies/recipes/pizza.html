Home Pizza

Description

If you are looking for a homemade pizza dough recipe that is perfect for beginners, you are in luck.
This highly rated recipe is very easy to make and outshines store-bought options.
Learn how to make the best pizza dough of your life using just a few ingredients, how to shape the dough properly, and get our best storage tips.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm water (45 degrees Celsius / 110 degrees Fahrenheit)

  • 1 packet (0.25 oz) active dry yeast

  • 1 teaspoon white sugar

  • 2 ½ cups bread flour

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 230 degrees Celsius

    (450 degrees Fahrenheit) and lightly grease a pizza pan.

  2. Step 2

    Pour warm water into a bowl; add yeast and sugar. Stir and

    let stand until creamy, about 10 minutes.

  3. Step 3

    Add flour, oil, and salt to the yeast mixture; beat until smooth.

    This can be done by hand or using a stand mixer with a dough hook.

  4. Step 4

    Let the dough rest for 5 minutes.

  5. Step 5

    Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and roll or shape it

    into a 30 cm circle.

  6. Step 6

    Transfer to the prepared pizza pan.

  7. Step 7

    Spread the crust with sauce and add toppings of your choice.

  8. Step 8

    Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown,

    15–20 minutes. Remove from the oven and let cool for 5 minutes

    before serving.
